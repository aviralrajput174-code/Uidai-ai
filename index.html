A simple verification routine

1. Pause. Don’t click or forward under pressure.

2. Find the original. Search for the source, author, date, and full context.

3. Cross-check. Compare independent, reputable sources—not copies of the same post.

4. Use official channels. Open the official app/site yourself instead of a message link.

5. Admit uncertainty. If evidence is missing, don’t treat the claim as confirmed.
